The Hunt · mission flow

Exorcists are dispatched when signs of a sin appear and must investigate. They know only its type and trauma questions. Locate its lair and execute it before catastrophe strikes. Find the palace: a sin creates a parasitic pocket dimension and cannot be slain outside. Return for debrief · RNR allowances.
{# ---- Tension / Pressure ------------------------------------------ #}

Tension · Pressure

Tension
Slash when a scene passes, or a '1' is rolled on risk (once per scene only). When it fills: increase pressure by 1, clear tension, then make a GM move.
Pressure
Slash when tension fills out, degrading the situation. Pressure increases also slash every exorcist's hooks and raise every sin's execution threshold.
